What is Rubber Duck Debugging?

It's a method where you explain your code, line by line, to a rubber duck (or any inanimate object). By forcing yourself to articulate the problem, you often find the solution!

Getting Started:

  • 🦆 Create your first rubber duck
  • 🎨 Choose a color that inspires you
  • 📝 Give it a name and description
  • 💡 Start explaining your code problems
  • ✨ Watch the magic happen!

Pro Tip: The more detailed your explanation to the duck, the more likely you are to spot the bug!
